## 🗄️ Database Setup

### ⚠️ IMPORTANT: Add Vercel KV for Persistent Storage

Your app is deployed but needs Vercel KV database:

1. Go to: https://vercel.com/md-jabed-pucs-projects/ph-demo
2. Click **"Storage"** tab
3. Click **"Create Database"**
4. Select **"KV"** (Key-Value)
5. Name: `products-db`
6. Click **"Create"**
7. **Redeploy** your project

**After adding KV:**

- ✅ Products persist permanently
- ✅ No data loss
- ✅ Free tier (256MB)
